# Project history

In 2024 the team began documenting customer questions and scheduling details in separate files. Staff answered common questions in prose and maintained dates in spreadsheets. The same topic could appear in several documents with different levels of detail.

## A change in the process

The team then introduced a review step before publishing updated schedules. A reviewer checked the technology node, process flavor, and dates against the original spreadsheet. If a value was unclear, the reviewer contacted the schedule owner before publication. This step reduced the chance that an incomplete row would appear as a confirmed date.

## Lessons learned

The team learned that an answer sometimes depends on several sentences of context. A question such as "Why was the review step introduced?" does not occur verbatim in this document. The relevant passage is the paragraph above. Keep enough surrounding text in a chunk so a later retrieval system can find and use that evidence.
